Cardholder agreement
Effective July 1, 2026 · LotteryCards Prepaid Mastercard
1. About your card
This prepaid Mastercard is issued to disburse state lottery winnings. It is not a credit card and not a bank account. Funds are held in FDIC-insured custodial accounts.
2. Using your card
You may use the card anywhere Mastercard debit is accepted, in mobile wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay), and in payment apps such as Venmo, Cash App, and PayPal.
3. Fees
No monthly fee. No fee at Chase ATMs. Out-of-network ATM withdrawals: $2.50 plus operator fees. Card replacement: free.
4. Lost or stolen cards
Lock your card instantly in the app. Report loss within 2 business days and your maximum liability is $50 under Mastercard Zero Liability.
5. Disputes
Contact the Support Crew within 60 days of a statement error. We will investigate within 10 business days and provisionally credit qualifying disputes.
6. Privacy
Your data is used to service your card and is never sold. Aggregate, de-identified insights may be shared with your state lottery. See the full privacy notice at lotterycards.com/legal.
This in-app summary is provided for convenience. The full agreement was delivered when your card was issued and is available in Settings or by mail on request.
