The friction of cash

Till limits cap what retail can pay. Armored transport, counting, and reconciliation make every cash dollar expensive to move — while mobile payment use has nearly tripled since 2018.

The friction of checks

Print, mail, wait, deposit, clear — weeks of delay for an instrument younger winners may never have used. Check payments are declining in both number and value.

The friction of ACH

Collecting routing numbers at a claim counter is slow and error-prone, settlement takes days — and it excludes every winner without a bank account.

The phone reaches everyone
Nearly 9 in 10 U.S. households have a smartphone — including 72% of households with no bank account at all. A virtual prepaid card reaches the winners a check frustrates and ACH can't serve.

Sources: Federal Reserve Diary of Consumer Payment Choice (2025) · Federal Reserve Payments Study (2025) · FDIC National Survey of Unbanked and Underbanked Households (2023).
